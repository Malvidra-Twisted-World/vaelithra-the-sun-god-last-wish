# Part V: What the Sun Left Behind

> *"Some inherit a name. Some inherit a promise. And some... become the reason that promise endures."*

Silence. For the first time since the Avatar of Kael'zar appeared, there are no more explosions. No more raging fire — only the crackle of embers still consuming the wreckage, the slow groan of collapsing timber, and the breathing of everyone who survived.

## Ashes Beneath the Sun

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Ash drifts slowly across House Ra's courtyard. The sun that had vanished behind smoke finally breaks through the clouds again, its light falling across grounds now scarred with wounds. The cherry trees are scorched black. Parts of the estate have collapsed. Cracks run through every remaining wall. Yet House Ra is still standing. The silence stretches long, as though no one can quite find the words. Anthony slowly sinks to his knees, the broken remains of his sword still in hand. He looks at the house he's protected his whole life — then laughs, quietly, a laugh full of relief. "...Still standing."

**Lady Miji** comes to sit beside him without a word, taking his hand. Anthony closes his eyes. *"...Thank the gods."* Soon after, the **Chief of Soyo Gumi** limps over to join them, his body covered in wounds, his golden haori scorched in places. He says nothing at first — he simply sits beside Anthony, and the two men look together at the ruined estate. After a moment, the Chief smiles faintly. *"...Your house."* He sighs. *"...Stubborn."* Anthony laughs quietly. *"...Its owner too."* For the first time since the battle began, the two men laugh together.

Elsewhere in the courtyard, **Lambda** runs to **Fradonic** and throws her arms around him without a word. He's still stunned, still trying to understand the power now running through his body — but slowly, he returns her embrace. *"...I'm sorry."* Lambda shakes her head firmly. *"Don't apologize."* Her voice trembles. *"...Just don't ever leave me."* Fradonic only smiles and holds her tighter.

***Aftermath.*** Give the party a chance to help the wounded, tend to survivors, speak with House Ra's household, or simply sit with the quiet after the battle. Let them feel that they truly did save this house.

***Sera's Farewell to the Vault.*** Meanwhile, Sera walks alone to the family vault. No one stops her. She lifts the now-empty wooden chest and slowly closes its lid — the sound echoing softly in the room. There is no more Solar Crest inside it. No more light. Only an empty chest that House Ra's family has guarded for hundreds of years. She studies it for a long moment, then bows — deeply. Watching from a distance, Anthony frowns. *"...Sera?"* She keeps her head bowed. *"...Thank you."* Anthony smiles, confused. *"...For what?"* Sera slowly lifts her head, her silver gaze carrying something entirely different than before — full of gratitude. *"...For centuries..."* *"...House Ra never once betrayed the amanah entrusted to them."*

Anthony laughs quietly. *"...Amanah?"* He looks at the empty chest. *"...I never even knew what I was actually guarding."* Sera nods. *"...I know."* *"...And that's exactly why..."* *"...Ra chose your family."* The room falls quiet again. Everyone now turns to look at Sera — the Chief stands, Lady Miji moves closer to Anthony, and Fradonic and Lambda step into the vault along with the party. Sera looks at each of their faces in turn, then back at the empty chest. *"...All this time..."* *"...you all believed House Ra was guarding a relic."* She shakes her head slowly. *"...No."* She closes the chest gently. *"...House Ra..."* *"...was guarding time itself."* No one understands what she means. Sera turns to Fradonic, her gaze soft, full of pride. *"...The Solar Crest was never the goal."* *"...It was only a vessel."* *"...A vessel Ra created..."* *"...to protect something far more precious."* She steps closer to Fradonic, then kneels — for the first time not as House Ra's attendant, but as a Human Vaelithra. She bows her head. *"Sun Ray VII."* *"Fradonic Ra."* *"From today..."* *"...I entrust the Vaelithra into your keeping."*

The room falls silent again. Fradonic stares at her, confused. *"...Vaelithra?"* Sera nods. *"...Yes."* She looks around at everyone present, then draws a long breath. *"...It's time you all knew..."* Her gaze lifts to the now-clear sky, sunlight spilling gently through the vault's window onto the empty chest that has finally finished its duty. She closes her eyes and whispers: *"...The Sun God's last wish."* Silence fills the room once more, and for the first time, a story kept for centuries is finally about to be told.

## The Sun God's Last Wish

The family vault is quiet again. The old wooden chest sits empty. The Solar Crest that House Ra guarded for centuries has finished its task. No more light. No more artifact. Only a simple chest, and a promise finally kept.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Sera rises slowly, her silver gaze sweeping the whole room — Anthony, Lady Miji, the Chief of Soyo Gumi, Lambda, Fradonic, and all of you. She smiles — the same smile that has always marked House Ra's attendant, but this time filled with relief, as though a weight carried for centuries has finally been set down. She draws a long breath, then begins to speak. "There's something... you all need to know."

***The Guardian of Time.*** *"Long before Dewanegara existed..."* Sera says, *"...the world endured a war that lasted thousands of years. A war between the Gods... and the Demons."* No one interrupts. *"In the middle of that war... there was one God who never raised a weapon."* She closes her eyes. *"His name..."* *"...was Vaeil."* *"God of Time."* *"Keeper of the Archive."* Fradonic frowns. *"...Archive?"* Sera nods slowly. *"The Archive is a sacred relic."* *"A place..."* *"...that safeguards the flow of time."* *"As long as the Archive exists..."* *"...every event continues to flow as it should."*

***The Greatest Taboo.*** Sera's smile fades. *"But..."* *"...Vaeil did something even the gods never dared to do."* The room falls silent. *"...He rewrote history."* Anthony freezes. The Chief lifts his head. Sera continues: *"That war..."* *"...was never truly won."* *"No side was destroyed."* *"No side truly lost."* *"Vaeil..."* *"...changed the course of time itself."* Silence fills the room. *"The world you know today..."* *"...is the world..."* *"...Vaeil chose."*

***The Price.*** *"But..."* *"...not everyone forgot the history that was changed."* Sera's gaze drifts to the window, out toward the still-smoking city of Atrakaj. *"Beings who stand equal to the Gods..."* *"...still remember the old world."* *"The Gods knew what Vaeil had done."* *"So did the Demons."* *"And..."* *"...they all wanted the Archive."* The room falls silent again.

***Ra's Promise.*** Sera looks at the empty wooden chest, running her hand gently across its surface. *"Vaeil knew..."* *"...the Archive would never truly be safe."* *"So..."* *"...he made one final sacrifice."* She grips the pendant at her throat. *"He shattered his own soul."* *"...and entrusted the fragments..."* *"...to those who would one day guard them."* Fradonic falls silent. Anthony stares at the empty chest. Sera smiles again. *"Before he vanished..."* *"...Vaeil asked one friend for help."* She looks at the sun emblem carved into the vault's wall. *"Ra."* *"The Sun God."* *"...received one of those fragments."* The room falls silent once more. *"He sealed it..."* *"...inside the Solar Crest."* *"Then entrusted it..."* *"...to House Ra."* Anthony slowly lowers his head. *"...So..."* *"...all this time..."* Sera nods. *"...Your family wasn't guarding a relic."* She looks at Fradonic. *"...You were guarding a promise."*

***The Sun God's Last Wish.*** Tears begin to fall down Sera's cheeks, though her smile never fades. *"The last day I saw Ra..."* *"...he didn't speak of the war."* *"...he didn't speak of the Gods."* *"...he didn't speak of the world."* She looks at Fradonic. *"...He only said..."* The room grows completely still.

Sera smiles, still crying. *"'...that I kept my promise to my friend.'"* Silence blankets the whole room. No one can find a single word to say.

***The New Keeper.*** Sera walks slowly to Fradonic and removes the spineless open-book pendant from her own neck, placing it into his open palms. *"Sun Ray VII."* *"Fradonic Ra."* *"From today..."* *"...that fragment of a soul has chosen you."* *"...And my duty..."* She glances toward the empty chest. *"...is complete."*

***The Next Keeper.*** Fradonic still holds the pendant, unsure what to say. After a long silence, he finally speaks. *"...If my duty now is to guard the Vaelithra..."* He looks at Sera. *"...then what am I supposed to do?"* Sera doesn't answer right away. She looks out the window instead, watching the evening sun begin to set, its light falling across House Ra's battle-scarred courtyard. After a moment, she smiles. *"For centuries..."* *"...I only had one duty."* *"Waiting."* She turns back to Fradonic. *"From today..."* *"...that duty is yours."* Fradonic grips the pendant tighter. *"...But I don't even know where to start."* Sera nods slowly. *"...That's right."* *"...Because no one has ever walked that path before."*

The room falls silent again. Sera raises her right hand and traces a small circle in the air with one fingertip. Silver light gathers into a constellation, and among the countless points of light, the silhouette of an owl slowly takes shape, wings spread mid-flight. Sera gazes at it for a long moment, as though remembering someone long lost. *"...One day."* *"...you'll meet someone."* Fradonic studies the constellation. *"...Who?"* Sera doesn't answer immediately, her silver eyes still fixed on the glowing owl. *"...I don't know..."* *"...whether he's still alive."* Silence. *"...I don't know..."* *"...whether he still remembers who he is."* Anthony and the Chief exchange a glance — neither understands what Sera means. She continues: *"...But I believe."* Her gaze turns to the darkening orange sky. *"...The Archive..."* *"...always brings together those who walk the same path."*

Fradonic tightens his grip on the pendant. *"...What's his name?"* Sera finally smiles — gently, full of hope. *"...Lunaris."* A pause. *"...Lunaris Thoht."* The room falls silent again — the name sounds foreign to everyone present. Sera watches the owl constellation slowly fade. *"...If one day..."* *"...you meet a man..."* *"...who chooses to close his eyes..."* *"...so he doesn't have to feel the world's wounds..."* She turns to Fradonic, her gaze calm. *"...walk beside him."* Fradonic frowns. *"...Why?"* Sera smiles faintly. *"...Because..."* *"...the path you'll have to walk..."* She looks toward the sun setting behind House Ra. *"...cannot be walked by either of you alone."* The vault falls quiet once more. Fradonic nods slowly. Though he doesn't yet understand everything Sera has said, he holds onto every word.

Sera steps closer and rests her fingers on the Vaelithra pendant now in Fradonic's hands. *"...Find him."* *"...And when the time comes..."* She pauses, as though choosing her final words with care. *"...you'll understand..."* *"...why Kael'zar didn't come here to kill you."* Silence. Fradonic nods, slowly. His expression is no longer confused — only resolute. Sera smiles. *"...Farewell, Guardian."* *"...May time always favor you."* Outside House Ra, the sun finally sets. But for the first time, Fradonic feels that his true journey has only just begun.

## Epilogue: Beneath the Same Sun

Several days have passed since the battle at House Ra. The fires that consumed most of the courtyard have long since gone out. Collapsed buildings are being rebuilt. The scorched cherry trees have been pruned and tended back to life. The Gyokko Gumi help with House Ra's reconstruction, while the Nisshoku Gumi continue guarding Atrakaj until the whole city has recovered. Scars from the battle remain visible. But life goes on.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Morning sun once again shines over House Ra. New lanterns hang along every corridor. Servants bustle past carrying flowers, decorative cloth, and trays of food. Parts of the house are still held up by bamboo scaffolding. But today, House Ra is filled with laughter again. Today isn't about war. Today is a wedding.

***The Wedding.*** The whole of House Ra's guest list fills the courtyard once more — this time with no screaming, no fire, no fear. **Anthony Ra** stands tall in his golden Haori as Sun Ray I. Beside him stands **Lady Miji**, smiling since the moment the sun rose. The **Chief of Soyo Gumi** attends as guest of honor. Every Sun Ray, Nisshoku, and Gyokko Commander fills the seats prepared for them, and at the very front sits a place of honor reserved for the party. Anthony approaches Fradonic and straightens the collar of his son's Sun Ray VII Haori. *"...If you'd postponed your wedding because of that demon..."* Anthony says with a small smile, *"...that would mean he won."* Fradonic smiles back. *"...I understand."* Anthony claps his son's shoulder. *"...Go."* *"...Don't keep your bride waiting."*

***A New Lady of House Ra.*** **Lambda** walks slowly toward the altar, dressed in a red kimono embroidered with gold thread, a white Haori bearing House Ra's crest draped across her shoulders. Lady Miji stands beside her, eyes already misting. *"...So beautiful."* Lambda laughs softly. *"...Thank you..."* Miji takes both her hands. *"...From today..."* *"...welcome home."* Lambda nods, tears sliding down her cheeks.

***Beneath the Same Sun.*** The ceremony is simple — not grand, but warm, held in front of family, friends, and everyone who risked their lives to protect House Ra. Fradonic takes Lambda's hands in his own. For the first time, he speaks not as a Sun Ray, not as House Ra's heir — only as a man. *"...I promise."* *"...Wherever this road takes me."* He smiles. *"...I'll always come home."* Lambda returns his smile. *"...Then..."* *"...I'll always be waiting for you."* Applause fills House Ra's courtyard. For the first time since the battle, laughter rings out once more.

***Farewell.*** As evening approaches, the guests begin taking their leave. Carriages depart House Ra one by one. The party prepares to continue their own journey. Before they go, Sera approaches them, once again dressed in her attendant's clothes, as though nothing at all happened days ago. She smiles gently. *"Thank you."* *"...For protecting our Guardian."* She opens a small wooden box, revealing several clear crystal shards, each palm-sized and uniquely shaped, silver light swirling within them like a constellation caught in glass. She takes one and gives it to each party member.

***Archive Fragment.*** *"This isn't a gift,"* Sera says with a smile. *"...It's a marker."* She looks at each player in turn. *"As long as you carry it..."* *"...the Archive will always remember..."* she clasps her hands over her heart, *"...that you were once part of this story."* The crystal feels warm to the touch. For a moment its inner light pulses gently, then quiets again. Sera doesn't explain further — she only smiles, as though certain that one day, they'll understand its true meaning themselves. This crystal is a **Chronoshard**; see *Appendix A: Magic Items* for its full properties.

***The Last Goodbye.*** As the party begins to leave House Ra, Fradonic calls out. *"Hey."* Everyone turns. He stands at the gate, Lambda's hand in his, both of them smiling. *"Thank you."* A brief silence. Then Fradonic nods once. *"...See you again."*

***Final Scene.*** Night begins to fall. House Ra grows quiet once more. Lanterns sway gently in the breeze. Anthony and Lady Miji sit together enjoying a cup of tea. Fradonic and Lambda walk slowly through the courtyard, now filled again with soft light. In the distance, Sera stands alone, watching the sun sink below the horizon. Then, slowly, she lifts her face. For the first time that night, the moon rises into the sky. Sera smiles — very quietly — and whispers: *"...Now..."* — a pause — *"...it's your turn."* The night wind drifts gently by. The lanterns sway. And beneath the same sky, one story ends, while another has only just begun.
