# Part II: The Amanah Awakens

> *"For the first time in my life... I've never felt this before."*

The banquet is over. Guests have retired to their rooms, and the cricket-song of late summer night has replaced the celebration's noise. Servants extinguish the lanterns one by one. House Ra finally settles into quiet — the last quiet it will know for a while.

## The Sun Stirs

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The night feels utterly peaceful. A warm summer breeze drifts through House Ra's gardens. In the distance, bamboo chimes softly against itself in the wind. For a moment, the whole world feels still. Then — **tok.** A quiet sound from somewhere inside the house. Not loud. Not quite placeable. Just one small sound, as if something has just stirred.

***Investigating the Sound.*** If the party goes looking for the source, they find **Anthony**, **Miji**, **Fradonic**, and **Sera** already searching too — no one knows where it came from. After a moment, it comes again — **tok** — closer this time.

***The Family Vault.*** Anthony stops in his tracks, his gaze fixed on an old wooden door deep within House Ra. It's unlike the estate's other doors: unguarded, unlocked, yet clearly well cared for. *"...The vault,"* he mutters. For the first time, his composure begins to slip.

***Sera's Reaction.*** As Anthony moves toward the door, Sera speaks up — still calm, but with an edge the party hasn't heard from her before. *"Lady Miji. Please stay with the guests."* Miji looks confused: *"Sera?"* Sera doesn't explain. She only watches the wooden door.

***Opening the Vault.*** Anthony opens the door slowly. The room inside is simple — no gold, no weapons, just a stone altar at its center. On the altar sits an old wooden chest that has stood there for generations. Anthony approaches it. **Tok.** The sound is coming from inside the chest. The room falls silent. Anthony rests a hand on its surface. His eyes widen. *"...Warm."* *"...This is the first time."* *"...In my whole life... I've never felt this before."*

## Dawn of a New Sun

The next morning, House Ra is busy again. Today is the day the family has long awaited: **Fradonic**'s induction as **Sun Ray VII**, followed by a private gathering to formally introduce **Lambda** as House Ra's future Lady. No one knows that today is also the day an ancient amanah will finally be passed on.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Morning sunlight bathes House Ra's courtyard. Servants have been busy since dawn. Formal kimonos hang neatly ready. Guests begin dressing in their finest. In the front courtyard, several Soyo Gumi members have already arrived to escort the family to headquarters. Today should be the best day of Fradonic Ra's life.

***Preparing for the Ceremony.*** Before departing, Fradonic asks the party to join him in House Ra's dojo. He looks far more nervous than he ever has facing a monster. *"Funny, huh,"* he says with a small smile. *"Fighting monsters is easier than meeting every senior officer in Soyo Gumi. Let alone introducing Lambda to my family."* He laughs quietly. Let the party talk with him here — it's their last chance to enjoy a peaceful moment with Fradonic before the story's tone changes.

***A Father's Request.*** Soon after, Anthony arrives, wearing his golden Haori as Sun Ray I. He studies Fradonic for a moment. *"Fradonic. Come with me for a minute."* Anthony leads him to a small pavilion overlooking House Ra's garden. If the party tries to follow, Anthony only smiles: *"Sorry. Family matter."*

***Father and Son.*** *(DM Note: The party is not present for this conversation. Use it to color how Anthony and Fradonic carry themselves for the rest of the chapter — nothing said here is information the party strictly needs, since Fradonic's promotion is confirmed publicly at the induction and the vault visit happens with the party present later. If a player wants to try eavesdropping anyway, the pavilion is in the open and Anthony deliberately chose it — a Dexterity (Stealth) check contested by Anthony's passive Perception 18 lets a player catch fragments of the conversation below.)* Anthony pours tea. Neither speaks for a while. Then Anthony begins: *"Today... you become Sun Ray VII."* He nods slowly. *"You earned it yourself. No family influence. No mercy from me."* A thin smile. *"I'm proud of you."* Fradonic falls silent — Anthony rarely says things like that. After a moment, Anthony continues: *"But... there's one responsibility... heavier than becoming a Sun Ray."* He looks toward the back of House Ra — toward the family vault. *"Tonight... after all the guests have gone. Come with me."* Fradonic nods, and doesn't ask further.

***A Mother's Touch.*** If a player wanders off during the preparations instead of staying with Fradonic in the dojo, they can find this scene in progress elsewhere in the house: Lady Miji helps Lambda into House Ra's formal family kimono. Lambda looks anxious. *"Am I... worthy of wearing this?"* Miji smiles gently. *"Not yet."* Lambda freezes. A beat later, Miji laughs. *"Because you're not part of the family yet."* She straightens Lambda's collar. *"But... I hope that one day you'll wear it without any nerves at all."* Lambda can only nod, holding back a smile. Behind them, Sera helps adjust Lambda's obi, studying her for a moment before saying quietly: *"This kimono was made... for the Lady of House Ra."* A player who catches that line and knows enough about House Ra's history may sense there's more weight to it than Sera lets on.

## The Gathering of the Sun

By midday, House Ra's main courtyard has become a hall of welcome. A red carpet stretches from the main gate to the family hall. The whole household stands ready to receive its honored guests. For House Ra, today isn't just an induction — it's the day two generations of Sun Ray stand side by side.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> One by one, footsteps fill House Ra's courtyard. The arriving guests are no ordinary nobles — they're names known across all of Dewanegara. Golden Haori. Black Haori. White Haori. Each bears a different emblem, yet all share one symbol: the Sun of Ra. Today, House Ra is the center of all Soyo Gumi's attention.

***Arrival of the Commanders.*** Let the party watch the Commanders arrive one by one — they don't need lengthy introductions, just an impression of presence: an old man in a golden Haori entering with unhurried steps; a woman in a black Haori arriving with several Nisshoku members; a man in a white Haori warmly greeted by Lady Miji. The party is free to observe or mingle before the ceremony begins.

***Arrival of the Chief.*** The courtyard falls suddenly silent. Every Commander stands at attention. Anthony steps forward. Moments later, the **Chief of Soyo Gumi** enters House Ra, and everyone bows — Anthony first, followed by every Commander present.

***The Seventh Sun.*** Anthony carries a long wooden box holding a golden Haori that has never been worn. He lifts it out and stands before Fradonic. *"Fradonic Ra. Step forward."* Fradonic does. Anthony studies his son for a long moment — then, with his own two hands, drapes the Haori of Sun Ray VII across Fradonic's shoulders.

## The Last Promise

Once the induction concludes, the guests settle into a midday feast House Ra has prepared. Laughter and conversation fill the courtyard again. For most guests, this is simply a celebration. For Anthony Ra, the day has only just begun.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Anthony rises from his seat and lifts his teacup. "Thank you — for the presence of family and friends." He turns to Fradonic, his expression suddenly far more serious. "Fradonic. Come with me." Anthony bows to the Chief of Soyo Gumi. "Chief. My apologies — there's a family matter I must attend to." The Chief nods. "Go. Today belongs to your family." Anthony walks toward House Ra's inner sanctum. Fradonic follows.

***Fradonic's Request.*** Before entering the corridor toward the family quarters, Fradonic stops and turns to the party. *"If you don't mind..."* he says with a small smile. *"...I'd like you to come too."* Anthony says nothing — he simply keeps walking. His silence can be taken as consent.

***The Family Vault.*** Anthony leads the group to a small room deep within House Ra, far plainer than the main hall — no gold, no weapons, no House Ra crest. Only a stone altar. On it sits an old wooden chest, simple almost to the point of seeming unworthy of something guarded for hundreds of years.

## The Chosen Keeper

The vault falls silent. At its center stands the plain stone altar, and on it the same old, whitish-brown wooden chest — no carvings, no family crest, no lock or chain. For hundreds of years, the chest has passed from one Head of House Ra to the next. No one has ever managed to open it.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Anthony stands before the stone altar, his gaze fixed on the wooden chest he's guarded for decades. He draws a long breath. "This is the amanah passed down to House Ra. Since our very first ancestor." His smile is thin — and bitter. "I don't know who made this chest. I don't know what's inside it. I've... never even managed to open it myself." The room falls silent. He turns to Fradonic, his gaze full of pride. "I believe... today isn't mine anymore." He steps back. "Fradonic. Open it."

***The Opening.*** Fradonic studies the chest for a moment. *"Father... what if I can't either?"* Anthony smiles faintly. *"If it's truly not yet time... the chest will simply stay closed. And House Ra will keep guarding it."* Fradonic nods and steps to the altar, reaching out both hands to touch the lid. Nothing happens — for several long seconds, the room stays silent. Then — **click.** A small sound from inside the chest. Everyone freezes. Anthony's eyes go wide. Lady Miji covers her mouth with both hands. Lambda unconsciously grabs the arm of the nearest party member. Slowly, the lid opens on its own.

***The Solar Crest.*** Inside the chest lies a golden sun emblem — a disc ringed with eight rays of light, a single golden amber stone set at its center. Despite its age, not a speck of dust marks its surface, as though time never touched it. Anthony's eyes fill with tears. *"...So... our ancestors never lied."* Fradonic lifts the **Solar Crest** from the chest. The instant his fingers touch the gold — **dum** — the whole room trembles gently. The amber stone begins glowing warm. **Dum.** Again. Not a sound — a pulse, like a heartbeat. Cracks of light spread through the amber, not as damage but as a pattern resembling an open book without a spine, visible only as light — the Solar Crest's surface stays perfectly intact. Anthony steps back without meaning to. *"...This has never happened."* *"...For decades... this heirloom has never reacted to anyone."* Fradonic stares at the Crest in confusion, feeling warmth spreading slowly through his whole body. Behind the group, Sera watches without blinking — for the first time, her calm expression fully breaks. She whispers, barely audible over the pulsing light: *"...At last. You've found your new Keeper."* A player who succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check catches the words; everyone else only sees her expression break.

## The Guardian's Oath

The vault is still filled with the Solar Crest's warm light, its gentle pulse growing steadily stronger. No one dares move. Fradonic still holds the Crest in both hands, feeling a warmth unlike anything he's felt before — a warmth that is slowly, subtly changing.

**BOOOOOOOM!!** — the whole of House Ra shudders violently. Stone walls tremble. Dust falls from the ceiling. Screams echo from the front courtyard. Anthony draws his katana. *"What's happening?!"* Before anyone can answer, Sera steps forward — for the first time since the party has known her, she walks past Anthony, past Lady Miji, past Fradonic, and stands directly in front of the vault's door, her back to the whole room.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Hot wind begins gusting into the room. The oil lamps go out, one by one. Sera slowly removes the apron she's worn all this time. She folds it neatly and sets it on a small table. No panic. No fear. Only stillness. She closes her eyes and draws a long breath. "It's been... quite a long time... living this life." Slowly, she opens her eyes again. Her silver gaze now radiates a soft light. She touches the open-book pendant at her throat, and white-gold light begins to flow from it. The simple kimono she wears slowly transforms into a long white robe etched with golden light. Her black hair begins to drift, untouched by any wind.

The room falls silent. Sera turns slowly to face Anthony, then bows deeply and formally. *"Lord Anthony. Lady Miji. Thank you... for guarding this amanah for hundreds of years."* Anthony freezes. Lady Miji can only stare, speechless.

Sera then turns to Fradonic, her expression far gentler. She bows again — deeper this time. *"Sun Ray Fradonic Ra. Successor of House Ra. The newly chosen Keeper."* She smiles — for the first time, not the smile of an attendant, but of someone who has waited centuries for this moment. *"Allow me... to introduce myself once more."* She lays her right hand over her heart, directly over the open-book pendant. *"My name... is Sera."* A pause. *"...Human Vaelithra."*

***The Reveal.*** The room goes quiet — no one understands what the name means. Only Anthony frowns slightly. *"...Vaelithra?"* Sera nods. *"I am the last keeper of the amanah entrusted by Ra, the Sun God. And today... that amanah has finally found its next guardian."*

Before Fradonic can respond — **BOOOOOOOOOOOM!!** — a far more devastating explosion rocks all of House Ra. This time it isn't just the house — the whole city of Atrakaj shakes with it. Red fire rises in the distance. The once-clear sky begins turning dark red. Sera closes her eyes, as though she already knows who has arrived. She opens them again, her gaze now filled with sorrow. *"...He's here."* *"...Kael'zar."* For the first time, the name is spoken aloud.

## Ending the Chapter

The sky over Atrakaj is burning, and the name Sera has kept silent for centuries now hangs in the open air. Whatever the Solar Crest has just done to Fradonic, there's no time left to understand it — something far larger has already arrived at House Ra's doorstep.
