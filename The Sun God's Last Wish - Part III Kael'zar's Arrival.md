# Part III: Kael'zar's Arrival

> *"Ra... at last... I've found you."*

The explosion that shook House Ra didn't come from within the house — it came from the heart of Atrakaj itself. Every building trembles. Distant screams rise from the city. Anthony throws open the vault door, and heat washes over everyone inside.

## The Burning Sky

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The sky has changed. Black clouds spiral into a massive vortex above Atrakaj. Sunlight is blotted out — yet the sky doesn't darken. Instead, it burns a brilliant, glowing red. Small fires begin falling like rain. In the distance, a colossal blaze splits the heart of the city in two. Screams rise from every direction. And for the first time, House Ra witnesses something none of them ever imagined: a towering, giant figure standing in the middle of the city, taller than the surrounding buildings, its body wreathed in reddish-black flame. Two great horns curve back from its head. Its red eyes sweep across the city — as though searching for something.

***The Chief Takes Command.*** The **Chief of Soyo Gumi** steps forward immediately. Every Commander snaps to attention — no panic, no hesitation, only orders. *"Gyokko. Evacuate every civilian."* The Gyokko Commanders move at once. *"Nisshoku. Secure the entire perimeter."* The Nisshoku Commanders draw their weapons. The Chief turns to the Sun Rays. *"With me."* Anthony nods immediately, and every Sun Ray moves to head for the city center.

Anthony is about to move when Sera raises a hand. *"Don't."* The room falls silent again. Anthony looks at her. *"...Why?"* Sera is still staring at the colossal figure standing far off in the city center, her silver gaze filled with sorrow. *"That..."* she says, drawing a slow breath, *"...isn't its true body. That's only an Avatar."* The Chief frowns. *"Avatar of who?"* For the first time, Sera speaks a name that has never once been recorded in Dewanegara's history. *"Kael'zar."* *"Wrath Incarnate."*

The room falls silent. Before anyone can ask further, the Avatar slowly raises its head. Though it stands kilometers away, everyone can feel — unmistakably — that it is looking directly at House Ra. Then a voice deep enough to shake the entire sky rolls across the city:

> [!info] Read Aloud
> **"RA..."** — fire flares up across every corner of the city — **"...AT LAST..."** — every window in House Ra shatters — **"...I'VE FOUND YOU."**

## The Wrath Incarnate

Silence blankets House Ra. No one moves. In the distance, the **Avatar of Kael'zar** still stands amid the flames consuming part of Atrakaj — but it doesn't advance a single step. Its red gaze is fixed on one person alone: **Fradonic Ra**, and the Solar Crest in his hands.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Hot wind sweeps across House Ra's courtyard. The once-blue sky has turned deep red. Ash drifts down like snow. Every member of Soyo Gumi stands in formation. In the front line stand seven golden Haori. Seven Suns. Behind them, the Nisshoku and Gyokko Commanders brace for whatever comes next. But the Avatar doesn't move. It only raises one hand. Red flame gathers in its palm. Then the entire sky rumbles with its voice.

> **"Heir of Ra."** A pause. **"Surrender..."** — the flame in its palm coils into a black sun — **"...that inheritance."** **"And this city..."** — it turns its gaze across Atrakaj — **"...will remain standing."**

No one answers. Anthony steps forward — one step. His golden Haori snaps in the wind. He draws his katana, the ring of steel cutting through House Ra's courtyard. He raises the blade, its point leveled squarely at the Avatar of Kael'zar, his gaze showing not a trace of fear.

***Roleplaying Anthony.*** *"As long as my family still breathes,"* Anthony says, drawing a slow breath as he takes his fighting stance, *"...that inheritance... will never fall into your hands."*

The Avatar studies Anthony for a moment, then laughs — not a harsh laugh, but one strangely full of pity. **"...Sun Ray."** **"...you're all the same."** Anthony frowns. *"...What do you mean?"* The Avatar shakes its head slowly. **"Ra..."** — red flame fills the sky — **"...always chooses humans..."** **"...too stubborn to ever give up."** It turns its gaze to Fradonic. **"...just like you."**

## Roll Initiative

The ground shudders again. Fire begins spreading toward House Ra. The Chief of Soyo Gumi raises a hand: *"SUN RAYS!"* All seven Sun Rays surge forward as one. *"PROTECT HOUSE RA!"* The Nisshoku Commanders draw their weapons; the Gyokko Commanders move to evacuate the household and its guests. Anthony doesn't so much as glance away from the Avatar of Kael'zar. *"Fradonic,"* he says, his voice calm. *"Protect your mother."* Then, for the first time, he smiles at his son. *"And prove..."* *"...that I wasn't wrong to choose you."* A moment later, Anthony blurs forward — fast as a flash of sunlight — and the battle begins.

***Running the Combat.*** This encounter is not one the party is meant to win outright — it's the opening blow of a battle that has already been decided against House Ra's defenders. **Anthony Ra**, **Vikram Sanjaya**, the six other Sun Rays, and every Commander of Nisshoku and Gyokko throw themselves at the **Avatar of Kael'zar** (full stats in *Appendix B: Creatures*) while the party protects Lady Miji, Lambda, and the evacuating household. Let the party see glimpses of the fight — Anthony's blade meeting the Avatar's claws again and again, Vikram rallying the line — but the Avatar is simply too strong. However the party spends this encounter, it ends the same way: one by one, Atrakaj's strongest warriors fall.

***Development.*** By the time the dust settles, Anthony has fallen, his sword — the one that has stood at his side for decades — broken in two. The six other Sun Rays lie defeated around him. The Chief of Soyo Gumi is still standing, but his breath comes ragged and slow. The Avatar of Kael'zar has not been stopped. It has only been waiting. Continue to *Part IV: The Heir of the Sun*.
