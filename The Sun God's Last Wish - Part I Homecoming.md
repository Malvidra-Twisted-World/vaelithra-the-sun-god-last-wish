# Part I: Homecoming

> *"Today isn't a day for battle. Today is a day to welcome someone home."*

**House Ra** stands proudly at the heart of the city of **Atrakaj** — one of the most influential families within **Soyo Gumi**, its estate closer to a noble compound than an ordinary home. The front grounds are filled with maple trees, stone gardens, a koi pond, and a dojo where the family trains; deeper inside sits the main hall, where formal introductions and dinners for honored guests take place.

The whole household is busy preparing for Fradonic's homecoming. Servants hurry back and forth with decorations, flowers, and gifts, readying the estate to welcome visitors from across Dewanegara. The party has arrived ahead of Fradonic himself, on his personal invitation, and is welcomed warmly by the family.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The afternoon sun spills across House Ra's wide courtyard. The air smells of warm tea and cedar wood, while servants bustle past carrying tables, lanterns, decorative cloth, and party preparations of every kind. In the distance, the sound of wood striking wood drifts from the dojo, mixed with the laughter of servants hanging decorations. Today isn't a day for battle. Today is a day to welcome someone home.

## Meeting Anthony Ra

Shortly after the party arrives, a man in his fifties approaches them. His hair is beginning to gray, but he still carries himself like the warrior he's always been — his gaze sharp, but calm. He wears a simple golden Haori marked with House Ra's sun emblem on the back.

This is **Anthony Ra** — **Sun Ray I**, Head of House Ra. He greets the party politely.

> "Welcome to House Ra."
>
> "Fradonic speaks of you often."
>
> "Thank you for looking after him during the mission."

Anthony isn't a man of many words, but it's clear he genuinely values the people who fought alongside his son.

## Meeting Lady Miji

Unlike Anthony, **Miji Ra** welcomes the party with unreserved warmth. The Silver Dragonborn ushers them inside without much ceremony.

> "Don't just stand out there!"
>
> "You're family's guests today!"

Miji quickly finds small tasks for the party while the servants finish preparing for the celebration.

***Helping House Ra.*** While waiting for Fradonic to arrive, the party is free to help with the estate's preparations — hanging lanterns, arranging the main hall, carrying guests' gifts, helping in the kitchen, clearing out storage, or setting up the dojo for the visiting guests. These small chores are a chance for the party to get to know House Ra and its staff before the story's stakes rise.

## The Lady's Attendant

Amid the bustle, the party repeatedly crosses paths with a young woman who seems to be helping with nearly everything Lady Miji needs. She wears a simple white kimono with a gray apron, her long black hair tied neatly back, and her calm, unreadable silver eyes give her a quietly composed presence. Around her neck hangs a silver necklace with a pendant shaped like an open book without a spine — an unfamiliar symbol, finely made.

Her name is **Sera**. Every servant in the house treats her with respect, and Lady Miji treats her like family rather than staff. Sera has long served as Miji's personal attendant, and seems to be responsible for nearly everything House Ra needs.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A woman approaches carrying a tray of warm tea. Her footsteps are nearly silent. With a gentle smile, she sets a cup before each of you before bowing politely. "Welcome to House Ra. Lady Miji is very grateful for your help." She smiles warmly. "My name is Sera."

***Roleplaying Sera.*** Sera always speaks softly and calmly. She rarely shows strong emotion, but always seems to know what someone needs before they ask. To the servants she's utterly dependable; to Lady Miji, she's far more than an attendant — she's been part of House Ra for a very long time.

***Helping Sera.*** If the party offers to help, Sera asks them to retrieve supplies from House Ra's storeroom — decoration boxes, one of Lady Miji's kimonos, gifts for the guests, family portraits bound for the main hall, or the family archive room, which needs tidying. She's happy to talk during the errand, and answers questions politely, though she rarely says much about herself.

***The House Ra Archive.*** The archive room holds generations of House Ra history: the swords of past Heads of House, family travel records, genealogies, and portraits of every past Head and Lady of the House, all kept in careful order despite some being centuries old.

***Portraits of the Ladies.*** A careful look at the row of Lady House Ra portraits reveals something strange: every portrait, from every generation, shows a woman standing just behind the Lady — a woman with a face nearly identical to Sera's. Her clothing changes with the era. Her face never does. Each portrait bears a small inscription: *Lady Ayame Ra & Sera. Lady Kaori Ra & Sera. Lady Misaki Ra & Sera...* all the way to the most recent: *Lady Miji Ra & Sera.*

If asked about the woman in the paintings, Sera only offers a thin smile: *"Every Lady of House Ra has always had an attendant."* If asked directly whether it's her in the older portraits, she answers just as calmly: *"Perhaps we simply happen to look alike."* She then returns to her work without further explanation, and never explains the meaning of the pendant she always wears — only that it's a treasured heirloom.

***Running Sera's Mystery.*** Don't lean on this too hard. Let the players find the strangeness in the portraits themselves rather than being pushed toward it. Sera's silver eyes and open-book-without-a-spine pendant are the signature traits of every Human Vaelithra — a subtle clue for players who have run earlier Vaelithra adventures. Nothing is lost if the party doesn't catch it now; the truth comes out naturally by the end of the adventure, in *Part V: What the Sun Left Behind*.

## Welcome Home

As afternoon fades toward evening, House Ra's preparations wind down. The decorations are nearly finished. The family and staff gather in the front courtyard after word arrives that Fradonic's carriage has entered the city gates. Soon, the sound of wheels grows close.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The sound of wooden wheels slowly fills House Ra's courtyard. A white horse pulls a simple carriage toward the main gate. When it stops, the door opens, and a long-haired man steps down slowly, his blue-green travel clothes still dusty from the road. His eyes search immediately for the one face he's missed most. Then he smiles. "I'm home."

***Reunion.*** **Lady Miji** is the first to reach Fradonic, sweeping her son into a tight embrace without a thought for the watching guests or staff. **Anthony** stays where he stands, composed as ever, though it's plain how relieved he is to see his son return safely. After a moment, he steps forward. *"Welcome back."* Fradonic bows respectfully. *"Thank you, Father."* Anthony nods. *"You came home just as you promised."*

***Greeting the Party.*** Fradonic greets each party member in turn like an old friend. Give every player a chance to describe their character's relationship with Fradonic from the mission that brought them here — light banter, a shared joke, a quick story from the road. This is a good moment to build the party's relationship with Fradonic before the story's tone shifts.

***Introducing Lambda.*** Once he's greeted everyone present, Fradonic turns back to the carriage and offers his hand inside. A red-skinned Tiefling steps down, black horns curving from her head, her plain clothing making it clear she isn't from a noble family. She's clearly trying to hide her nerves. Fradonic smiles at her: *"It's alright. They're a good family."* She nods, then approaches Anthony and Miji and bows respectfully. *"My name is Lambda Rospira."* The courtyard falls silent for a moment. Fradonic takes a breath, then speaks with resolve: *"Father. Mother. Lambda is the woman I want to marry."*

***Anthony's Response.*** Anthony studies Lambda for a long moment without a word — no anger, no visible joy, only the look of a father getting to know someone his son has chosen. After a few seconds, he nods. *"Welcome to House Ra. Come inside."* No questions. No objection. No judgment.

***Miji's Response.*** Unlike Anthony, Miji closes the distance immediately, taking both of Lambda's hands with a warm smile. *"That must have been an exhausting trip. From today... consider this house your home too."* Lambda looks briefly startled. *"T-thank you, Lady Miji..."* Miji just laughs. *"Lady? Call me Mom once you're used to it."* Lambda can only smile, embarrassed.

***Sera's Observation.*** Throughout the exchange, Sera stands quietly a few steps behind Lady Miji, her silver eyes occasionally drifting to Lambda — it's hard to tell what she's thinking. But when Fradonic takes Lambda's hand, Sera's mouth curves into a faint, almost invisible smile, as though she's been waiting a long time for this exact moment.

## The Welcome Banquet

As night falls, House Ra grows livelier still. Every decoration is finished. Lanterns are lit one by one, bathing the main courtyard in warm golden light. In the main hall, long tables are filled with dishes typical of Vostok. Tonight isn't a formal ball — it's a simple gathering for House Ra's family, Fradonic's friends, and the handful of honored guests who've already arrived.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The smell of warm food fills the main hall. Laughter from the servants mixes with the clink of teacups and sake. For the first time since arriving at House Ra, you can feel what it's like to be part of a family. Tonight — no missions. No war. No orders. Just a family, welcoming their son home.

***The Banquet.*** Anthony sits at the head of the table as Head of House Ra, with Miji beside him. Fradonic sits across from the party. Lambda still looks a little nervous, but slowly starts to enjoy herself. Sera helps the servants bring out food, occasionally sitting when Lady Miji insists she rest.

***Conversations.*** The party is free to talk with anyone during the meal. Good topics include Fradonic's experiences on the mission, how he and Lambda met, a brief history of House Ra, life as a member of Nisshoku or Gyokko Gumi, the coming induction ceremony, and the engagement preparations. Let the conversation develop naturally — not every piece of information needs to come out at once.

Anthony isn't much of a talker, but listens closely when engaged. To a Nisshoku Gumi character: *"Fradonic always says you can be relied on."* To a Gyokko Gumi character: *"Thank you for looking after my son during his duties."* Anthony is visibly proud of Fradonic, though he never overstates it.

Lady Miji is Anthony's opposite — she laughs often, teases Fradonic mercilessly, and does her best to ease Lambda's nerves: *"Fradonic used to cry whenever he lost a sword-fighting match as a kid,"* prompting an immediate *"Mom..."* from Fradonic while Miji just laughs.

For the first time, the party sees a different side of Fradonic — not on a mission, not in combat, just a son who's finally come home. He's noticeably more relaxed than usual, and joins in the laughter more than once.

Lambda is still nervous, listening more than speaking, but opens up gradually if the party draws her into conversation, sharing stories from her travels with Fradonic. She glances at him now and then with a small smile — it's clear the two of them are very close.

***The Empty Seat.*** One seat at the end of the table remains empty. If asked about it, Lady Miji only smiles: *"For a guest who hasn't arrived yet."* Anthony offers no comment. Sera glances at the seat for a moment before returning to her work. No further explanation is given.

***Closing the Night.*** Once the banquet ends, the servants begin clearing the tables. Lanterns sway gently in the night breeze. House Ra settles back into quiet. Fradonic walks Lambda through the estate, introducing her to a few more family members along the way. Anthony walks alone toward the back garden. If any player lingers near the garden or steps away from the group, they notice Sera standing apart from the festivities, gazing up at the night sky, her silver eyes fixed on the moon — for the first time, her smile fades. A player within earshot who succeeds on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check catches her whisper: *"...It's almost time."* Even without the words, her sudden stillness is plain to see.

## Ending the Chapter

The household settles for the night, the celebration's warmth still lingering in the lanterns and half-cleared tables. Nothing about the evening has hinted at what's coming — only Sera's private, unheard words in the dark suggest that House Ra's quiet homecoming is about to become something much larger.
