# Running the Adventure

## *The Weight of a Name*

> *"Some inherit a name. Some inherit a promise. And some... become the reason that promise endures."*

This is for the Dungeon Master. It contains a complete adventure — *Vaelithra V: The Sun God's Last Wish* — set in the present age of **Dewanegara**, in the city of **Atrakaj**, region of **Vostok**. It begins as a homecoming celebration and ends as the day House Ra's oldest secret finally comes due.

> **Recommended Level:** 10–12

> **Party Size:** 4–5 characters

## Overview

*Vaelithra V: The Sun God's Last Wish* is a one-shot built around a single day and a half in the life of one family. For most of the adventure there is no villain, no dungeon, and barely any combat — only a homecoming, a wedding to plan, and a quiet mystery hiding in plain sight among the portraits of House Ra. The party spends the first half of the story simply being welcomed into a family, and the second half watching that family's centuries-old, unspoken promise finally come due, all at once, in fire.

The party are not the heroes of this story so much as its honored guests. **Fradonic Ra**, freshly promoted to **Sun Ray VII**, invites his old companions home to celebrate — and unknowingly to witness the day his family's inheritance chooses him. When the **Avatar of Kael'zar** arrives to test whether that inheritance is real, the party's role shifts from wedding guests to House Ra's last line of defense: first protecting Fradonic while the power he's inherited overwhelms him, then standing with him as he turns to face it.

Here's an overview of what you'll find:

- **Running the Adventure.** This file: an overview of House Ra, Soyo Gumi, and guidance on running the adventure's themes.
- **Adventure Sites.** Five parts follow the party from Fradonic's homecoming, through the awakening of House Ra's amanah, to the Avatar's arrival and the final duel, and finally to the truth Sera has kept for centuries.
- **Appendices.** Two appendices describe the magic items and the creatures that appear in the adventure.

## Adventure Background

Long before Dewanegara as it stands today, the world was consumed by a war between gods and demons that lasted millennia — **the Sundering War**. Among the gods, one never raised a weapon: **Vaeil**, God of Time and Keeper of the Archive. To end the war without true victors or true casualties, Vaeil did something no god had ever dared: he rewrote the course of history itself. The world that exists today is the world Vaeil chose.

That act carried a price. Beings who stand beside the gods — other gods, and the greatest of the demons — still remember the world that was erased, and want the Archive that made the change possible. Knowing it could never be truly safe, Vaeil made one final sacrifice: he shattered his own soul into twelve fragments, known as the **Vaelithra**, and entrusted each one to someone who would one day guard it.

One fragment went to **Ra**, God of the Sun and Vaeil's oldest friend, as a final act of respect between them. Ra sealed the fragment into a golden disc ringed with eight rays of sunlight, set with a single amber stone at its center — the **Solar Crest** — and, knowing his own time in the mortal world was ending, entrusted it to the family he trusted most: **House Ra**. For generations, House Ra guarded the crest without ever learning what it truly was, passing a locked, unmarked chest from one Head of House to the next as nothing more than "an heirloom of the Sun God."

Before he vanished from the world, Ra left one more instruction — not with House Ra, but with **Sera**, the Human Vaelithra bound to the fragment inside the Solar Crest: that when someone finally arrived strong enough to protect what Ra himself no longer could, she was to give it to them, and tell them that Ra had kept his promise to his friend.

That day is the day the party arrives at House Ra.

### Adventure Outline

*Vaelithra V: The Sun God's Last Wish* has five parts.

*Part I*, "Homecoming," welcomes the party to House Ra as honored guests for Fradonic's return, his induction as Sun Ray VII, and Lambda Rospira's introduction to the family — and introduces Sera, House Ra's quietly uncanny attendant, whose face has appeared beside every Lady of House Ra for centuries.

*Part II*, "The Amanah Awakens," follows the induction ceremony and the first stirrings of the family vault, as a peti kayu that has never opened in living memory begins knocking from the inside — on the very night House Ra's amanah finally chooses its keeper.

*Part III*, "Kael'zar's Arrival," brings the Solar Crest's opening and the sky above Atrakaj catching fire, as the **Avatar of Kael'zar** descends on House Ra demanding "Ra's inheritance" and every Sun Ray who can still stand rises to meet him.

*Part IV*, "The Heir of the Sun," is the adventure's climax: first a desperate defense of Fradonic as the Solar Crest's power threatens to consume him before he can master it, then the full duel between Fradonic — reborn as the Heir of the Sun — and the Avatar, ending in a revelation about why Kael'zar really came.

*Part V*, "What the Sun Left Behind," is the aftermath: House Ra still standing amid the ashes, Sera's centuries-long vigil finally ending, the truth of Vaeil, Ra, and the Vaelithra fragments laid bare, and a wedding beneath a sky finally at peace.

## House Ra and Soyo Gumi

All player characters are current or former members of **Nisshoku Gumi** or **Gyokko Gumi**, the two branches of **Soyo Gumi** who once ran a mission alongside Fradonic Ra — the mission that earned him his recommendation to become **Sun Ray VII**.

**Soyo Gumi** is the umbrella organization of every warrior in Atrakaj, led by **Vikram Sanjaya**, Chief of Soyo Gumi, and enforced by seven elite Commanders bearing the title **Sun Ray** (Sun Ray I through VII), each wearing a golden Haori marked with the sun. Two subordinate branches operate beneath Soyo Gumi's command, each with its own identity and specialty:

| Branch | Focus | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Nisshoku Gumi** | Frontline defense and direct combat | Holds the perimeter so evacuation can happen safely; player characters may come from here. |
| **Gyokko Gumi** | Evacuation and rescue | Gets civilians out of harm's way first, combat second; player characters may come from here. |

In peacetime, Nisshoku and Gyokko operate as distinct branches with their own pride and identity. In crisis, Vikram calls all three groups to stand together "as Dewanegara" — not as separate factions, a moment the adventure's climax is built around.

**Anthony Ra** holds the title of **Sun Ray I** as Head of House Ra — one of the most influential families within Soyo Gumi. His son, **Fradonic Ra**, is inducted as **Sun Ray VII** over the course of this adventure, a title he earns entirely on his own merit, without the weight of his family name behind it.

## Running This Adventure

For most of this adventure, there is no threat at all — only a family welcoming its son home, and the quiet, unhurried business of watching two people fall in love, get engaged, and get folded into a household that already loves them. Don't rush Parts I and II. The warmth of House Ra is what makes Part III land.

### DM Tips

**Let the mystery of Sera stay a mystery.** Every generation of Lady House Ra has a painting with the same silver-eyed attendant standing behind her, wearing clothes that change with the era but a face that never does. Don't force the players to notice — if they do, let it sit unexplained until Part V. If they don't, nothing is lost; the truth arrives regardless when Sera chooses to tell it.

**Scene 13 ("The Inheritor of the Sun") is a protection encounter, not a boss fight.** The party's job is to keep Fradonic alive while the Solar Crest's power destabilizes inside him — he is not yet strong enough to fight back. Make this feel urgent and physically dangerous without making it feel like the party is meant to defeat the Avatar here; they are buying time, not winning.

**Scene 15 ("Wrath Against the Sun") is the real duel — but Kael'zar isn't trying to conquer House Ra.** He's testing whether Ra's chosen heir is real. Play him with genuine, almost curious patience: he pulls his punches more than once, lets Fradonic recover, and only escalates when the fight threatens to end too easily. When Fradonic proves himself, Kael'zar stops attacking outright — the fight was never about the Solar Crest at all, only about confirming that "the successor of Ra" truly exists. Let that reveal land as unsettling rather than triumphant: Kael'zar promises to return, without an avatar, next time.

**Anthony Ra shows love through action, not words.** He rarely says what he feels directly — "I'm proud of you" in Scene 6 is a rare and deliberate exception. Let his silences carry as much weight as his sentences.

**The empty seat at the banquet table (Scene 4) is a quiet, unexplained detail — leave it that way.** Lady Miji's "for a guest who hasn't arrived yet" and Sera's brief glance toward it are the only acknowledgment it gets until the truth comes out. Resist the urge to explain it early.

## Important NPCs

**Anthony Ra**, **Sun Ray I** and Head of House Ra, is a man in his fifties, hair beginning to gray but still standing like the warrior he's always been — composed, quiet, and sharp-eyed rather than distant. He rarely speaks more than he needs to, but listens carefully and is quietly, deeply proud of his son. He fights alongside every other Sun Ray against the Avatar of Kael'zar until his sword breaks beneath him.

**Lady Miji Ra**, a Silver Dragonborn and Lady of House Ra, is Anthony's opposite in nearly every way — warm, quick to laugh, and immediately, unreservedly welcoming to anyone Fradonic brings home. She's the one who puts Lambda at ease within minutes of meeting her, and the one who sits beside Anthony without a word once the fighting is finally over.

**Fradonic Ra**, House Ra's heir and newly-inducted **Sun Ray VII**, returns home from a mission with the party and with Lambda, the woman he intends to marry. He has no idea that his homecoming also happens to be the night House Ra's amanah finally awakens — or that surviving what comes next means bearing power his own body was never built to hold. Once he does, he becomes the **Heir of the Sun**, fighting beside the party for the rest of the adventure.

**Lambda Rospira**, a red-skinned Tiefling and Fradonic's fiancée, isn't from a noble family and is visibly nervous about meeting House Ra for the first time — until Lady Miji all but adopts her on the spot. She has no combat role in this adventure; she is the reason Fradonic keeps standing back up.

**Sera** appears to be nothing more than Lady Miji's devoted personal attendant — quiet, unfailingly attentive, and present in a painting beside every Lady of House Ra for as far back as the family archive goes. She is, in truth, the **Human Vaelithra** bound to the fragment of Vaeil's soul sealed within the Solar Crest, and has waited centuries at House Ra's side for the moment she finally gets to hand it — and the truth behind it — to someone strong enough to carry both. When that moment comes, she also carries House Ra's final thread forward: a hint of a man named **Lunaris Thoht**, someone Fradonic hasn't met yet, and won't for a while.

**Vikram Sanjaya**, Chief of Soyo Gumi, commands all seven Sun Rays and both subordinate branches. He is present as guest of honor for Fradonic's induction, and fights on the front line beside Anthony and the Sun Rays when Kael'zar's Avatar arrives — the one who calls the three branches to stand together "as Dewanegara" in the adventure's darkest hour.

**The Avatar of Kael'zar** descends on House Ra demanding "Ra's inheritance," burning through Atrakaj's strongest warriors with almost gentle inevitability. He isn't here to conquer, and he isn't the true Kael'zar — only a fragment of him, sent to answer one question: does Ra's successor actually exist? Everything he does in this adventure, including the violence, is in service of that test.

## Looking Ahead

Before the party leaves House Ra in the Epilogue, Sera gives each of them a **Chronoshard** — a small, unique crystal that isn't a reward so much as a marker, a way for the Archive to remember they were part of this. She also leaves Fradonic with a name he doesn't recognize yet: **Lunaris Thoht**, a man who "chooses to close his eyes so he doesn't have to feel the world's wounds." Neither thread pays off within this adventure — they're seeds for a future chapter of the Vaelithra Saga, and can be left as open, unresolved mysteries at the table.
