# Appendix A: Magic Items

*Vaelithra V: The Sun God's Last Wish* contains three magic items: the **Solar Crest**, House Ra's amanah, found and consumed over the course of *Parts II* and *IV*; the **Vaelithra Pendant**, transferred from Sera to Fradonic at the close of *Part V*; and the **Chronoshard**, a keepsake given to each party member in the Epilogue.

## Item Descriptions

> [!loot] The Solar Crest
> *Wondrous Item, Artifact (Requires Attunement)*
>
> A golden disc ringed with eight rays of sunlight, a single amber stone set at its center. Despite resting inside an unopened chest for centuries, it has never gathered a speck of dust, as though time itself refuses to touch it. The Solar Crest was forged by **Ra**, the Sun God, to seal away one of the twelve fragments of **Vaeil**'s soul — a **Vaelithra** — as a final act of trust between two old friends. Ra entrusted it to House Ra, who guarded it for generations without ever learning what it truly was.
>
> The Solar Crest cannot be attuned to at will — it chooses its bearer. It activates only for someone Ra's fragment recognizes as worthy to inherit it (in this adventure, **Fradonic Ra**, upon opening House Ra's family vault in *Part II: The Amanah Awakens*). Until that moment, it registers to any examination as nothing more than an old, inert family heirloom.
>
> ___
>
> ### A Vessel, Not a Prize
> The Solar Crest is not the true treasure it guards — it is only a container built by Ra to protect it. Once its chosen bearer proves strong enough to hold the fragment of Vaeil's soul directly (in *Part IV: The Heir of the Sun*, when Fradonic fully awakens as the **Heir of the Sun**), the Crest crumbles into motes of light and is consumed entirely, its power and the fragment within passing permanently into its bearer. The Solar Crest cannot be recovered, recreated, or used again after this point — this is intended, not a flaw. Its story ends the moment it finishes the purpose Ra built it for.
>
> ___
>
> ### Dormant Warmth
> While the Solar Crest remains sealed but not yet bonded, it radiates a faint, comforting warmth to anyone who touches it, and resists all attempts to force it open, damage it, or divine its contents by any means short of its true bearer's touch.
>
> ___
>
> ### Awakening
> The moment its bearer is determined, the Solar Crest begins transferring Ra's light into them over several minutes. During this time, the bearer cannot fight, gains vulnerability to all damage, and must be actively protected by their allies — a mortal body cannot safely absorb this much divine power all at once. If the transfer is interrupted by the bearer's death, the fragment returns to the Crest and the process must begin again from a full rest.

> [!loot] The Vaelithra Pendant
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*
>
> A silver necklace bearing a pendant shaped like an open book without a spine — the signature mark of every **Human Vaelithra**, a mortal permanently bound to one of the twelve fragments of Vaeil's soul. For centuries this pendant belonged to **Sera**, who wore it while bound to the fragment sealed within the Solar Crest. In *Part V: What the Sun Left Behind*, once her duty is complete, Sera passes the pendant — and the bond it represents — to **Fradonic Ra**.
>
> ___
>
> ### Mark of the Vaelithra
> While attuned to this pendant, your eyes take on a faint silver sheen, and creatures who have personally met a Human Vaelithra before have advantage on Insight checks to recognize you as one. You are immune to any effect that would erase your memories or alter your perception of history (though you are not immune to simply not knowing something).
>
> ___
>
> ### Keeper's Calling
> You always know the general direction of the **Infinite Archive**, though not the means to reach it. Once per long rest, you may cast *sanctuary* on yourself without expending a spell slot or material components.
>
> ___
>
> ### An Unfinished Duty
> The Vaelithra Pendant does not grant its wearer power so much as purpose — it marks them as one of the twelve who will, someday, be called upon to protect the Archive. What that duty entails beyond this adventure is left to future chapters of the Vaelithra Saga.

> [!loot] Chronoshard
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement)*
>
> A palm-sized crystal of flawless clarity, unique in shape and unlike any other. Within its transparent form drifts a constellation of tiny silver lights that move as though they belong to a living night sky. No two Chronoshards share the same shape. While holding it, the crystal feels slightly warm, as though responding to the presence of its bearer. Sera gives one to each party member at the close of *Part V: What the Sun Left Behind*, describing it not as a reward, but as a marker — proof that the Archive remembers they were part of this story.
>
> ___
>
> ### Dormant Power
> While attuned to this item:
>
> - You have advantage on Wisdom saving throws.
> - You have advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened.
> - You always know the direction of true north.
> - This item cannot be destroyed by non-artifact means.
>
> ___
>
> ### Echo of Time
> Whenever you finish a Long Rest, the silver lights within the crystal briefly rearrange themselves before returning to their endless dance. The effect has no known magical purpose.
>
> ___
>
> ### Hidden Truth
> The Chronoshard is one of several mysterious relics entrusted to those who have crossed paths with a Human Vaelithra. Its true purpose remains unknown.
