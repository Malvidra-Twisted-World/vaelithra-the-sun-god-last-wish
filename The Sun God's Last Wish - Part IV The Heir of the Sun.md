# Part IV: The Heir of the Sun

> *"Bloom, then. Heir of the Sun."*

**Anthony** has fallen, his sword broken beneath him. The six other Sun Rays lie defeated around him. The **Chief of Soyo Gumi** still stands, but his breath comes ragged and slow. All of House Ra's grounds have become a battlefield — trees burning, stone cracking under the force of the fight. No one imagined a power like this could exist.

## The Inheritor of the Sun

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The **Avatar of Kael'zar** advances slowly. No urgency. No anger. As though the outcome of this fight was already known to it from the start. With every step, the ground blackens. Grass turns to ash. The air grows thick with suffocating heat. It stops a few meters from Anthony. Its red gaze slowly shifts — not to Sun Ray I, not to the Chief — but to **Fradonic**, and the Solar Crest still in his hands.

> **"Give me..."** — the Avatar extends its hand — **"...Ra's inheritance."**

No one moves. Anthony struggles to rise, but his body no longer answers him — he can only grip the remains of his sword. *"Fradonic..."* *"...don't..."* Before he can finish, the Solar Crest pulses again — **DUM** — harder. **DUM** — faster. Golden light begins wrapping around Fradonic's entire body. His eyes close. His body trembles violently.

***The Awakening.*** The Solar Crest slowly lifts free of Fradonic's hands, hovering just in front of his chest. The light pouring from the amber stone becomes a sunburst so bright everyone is forced to shield their eyes. Then the light collapses into a single stream of energy and strikes Fradonic's chest. He screams. Light wraps his entire body. Silver dragon scales spread across both his arms. His eyes change — no longer blue, but blazing gold. Two dragon wings unfurl from his back, wreathed in sunlight rather than flame. His body begins to hover inches above the ground as he struggles to master the power now flooding into him — but it's too much. Too vast. He can't even stand upright.

***A Human Cannot Bear a God.*** Sera rushes to steady Fradonic's body, staring at the Solar Crest with open alarm. *"No..."* *"...too fast..."* She grits her teeth. *"A human body..."* *"...cannot hold the whole of Ra's light."* Fradonic collapses to his knees, breathing hard, his skin webbed with cracks of golden light — light escaping with every breath he takes.

The Avatar of Kael'zar smiles — for the first time, a smile full of satisfaction. **"Good..."** **"...bloom..."** **"...Heir of the Sun."** It raises both hands, and flame begins gathering across the sky. **"Only then..."** **"...can I finally kill you."**

***The Last Hope.*** Sera stands protectively in front of Fradonic and turns to the party, her expression showing something none of them have seen from her before: despair. *"Please..."* she says, clasping both hands together. *"...I can't protect him alone."* Her silver gaze moves from player to player. *"Protect Fradonic."* *"Give his body time..."* *"...to accept that light."* She turns back to Fradonic, her voice softening again. *"Don't fight the light."* *"...Accept it."* Fradonic closes his eyes, still kneeling, still struggling. Meanwhile, the Avatar of Kael'zar begins walking toward House Ra, each step bringing the fire closer.

## Roll Initiative

The fight begins. The party's goal here isn't to defeat the Avatar of Kael'zar — it's to keep **Fradonic** alive while he struggles to master the power of Ra newly awakened inside him. After several rounds, Fradonic finally masters a fraction of that power and is ready to join the fight himself.

***Running the Combat.*** This is a protection encounter, not a boss fight — Fradonic cannot yet fight back, so the party's job is to keep the **Avatar of Kael'zar**'s attention and damage off him long enough for the Solar Crest's power to stabilize (full stats for the Avatar in *Appendix B: Creatures*). Let this feel dangerous and physically demanding, but not winnable outright; the encounter ends on a timer, not a health bar. When it does, Fradonic rises and joins the party as an ally for the rest of the adventure — see his "Heir of the Sun" stat block in *Appendix B*.

## The Sun Rises Once More

The battle has raged for several minutes now. All of House Ra's grounds have become a sea of fire. Cherry trees burn. Nearby buildings begin to collapse. And still, no one retreats. The Gyokko Gumi continue evacuating civilians. The Nisshoku Gumi hold House Ra's perimeter, keeping the flames from reaching the fleeing townsfolk. At the front line, Sun Ray I through VI still struggle to rise despite their wounds. They have lost. But they have not surrendered.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Fradonic still kneels, his body still webbed with cracks of golden light — every breath sends sparks of sunlight escaping through them. The Solar Crest hovers before him, its light growing brighter, stronger, and harder to control by the second. The Avatar of Kael'zar keeps advancing. Unhurried. Unstopping. Every step burns the ground beneath its feet. There's no hatred on its face. No anger. Only conviction — that no one here can stop it.

***The Last Wall.*** **Chief Vikram Sanjaya** plants himself in front of Fradonic. His body is covered in burns, but he still grips his weapon. **Anthony** stands beside him. The six other Sun Rays retake their positions. Every Nisshoku Commander forms a second line. Every Gyokko Commander stands behind them. No one retreats. The Chief raises his weapon. *"Today..."* he says, drawing a long breath, *"...there is no Soyo."* He looks across every Commander present. *"...no Nisshoku."* His gaze turns to Gyokko. *"...no Gyokko."* Then he raises his weapon to the sky. *"Today..."* *"...we stand..."* *"...as Dewanegara."* A roar goes up from every Soyo Gumi member present, echoing across House Ra's grounds. Even the household servants take up spears and simple bows. No one wants to run. They choose, instead, to stand together.

***Wrath Incarnate.*** The Avatar of Kael'zar halts, studying every human now standing before it. For a moment, it says nothing. Then it laughs quietly. **"...Still..."** **"...human.""** It raises its right hand, and every fire across Atrakaj begins gathering into its palm — burning houses, trees, lanterns, even the flames still consuming House Ra's grounds, all pulled toward a single point. A black sun slowly forms above its palm. Sera's eyes go wide. *"...No..."* *"...everyone fall back!"* Anthony shouts immediately: *"HOLD!"* But it's too late. The Avatar closes its fist. The black sun detonates. A massive wave of fire sweeps across House Ra's entire grounds. The Chief is thrown dozens of meters. Sun Ray II through VI are hurled into buildings. The Nisshoku and Gyokko Commanders try to hold the line, but one by one they're beaten back. Anthony still stands, driving what remains of his sword into the ground, his whole body shaking against the pressure — until, finally, the blade snaps. Anthony collapses to his knees.

***The Chosen Sun.*** Silence returns to House Ra's grounds. The Avatar resumes its advance. Now, no one stands in its way — only the party, Fradonic, and Sera. It reaches its hand toward the Solar Crest. **"It's over."** But just before its fingers touch the artifact, the Solar Crest blazes with light far brighter than before. All of Atrakaj's sky lights up gold once more. The Avatar of Kael'zar stops in its tracks. For the first time, its expression changes — not to anger, but to shock. The Solar Crest slowly rises into the air. Then the cracks of light across the amber stone split fully open, and every last bit of light within it flows into Fradonic. *"AAAAAAARGHHHH!!"* His body lifts off the ground. Silver dragon wings spread wide, sunlight flowing between the scales. His eyes open fully — a pair of gold eyes now staring straight at the Avatar of Kael'zar. The Solar Crest begins to crumble — not from any attack, but because every ounce of its power has now passed into Fradonic. Its golden fragments dissolve into motes of light, drawn into his body. Sera watches, her eyes beginning to well with tears. She smiles. *"...At last."* *"...House Ra's duty is complete."* Fradonic slowly lifts his head, still disoriented, still not fully understanding what's happening — but now, he can feel the sun. Not in his hands. Inside himself.

The Avatar of Kael'zar smiles — wider than before. **"...Good."** **"...Now..."** It raises both hands as fire fills the sky once more. **"...at last I can fight..."** It points at Fradonic. **"...the Successor of the Sun."**

## Roll Initiative

Fradonic has now claimed a portion of Ra's power. He joins the fight as an ally NPC alongside the party. The final battle against the Avatar of Kael'zar begins.

## Wrath Against the Sun

The battle finally begins. House Ra's grounds, once filled with laughter and sunlight, are now a battlefield. Fire consumes every corner of the courtyard. Cherry trees collapse one after another. The air is thick with ash and sparks. And yet, in the middle of that inferno, one family still stands: **House Ra**.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The Avatar of Kael'zar looks at Fradonic. Not at any other human. Not at House Ra. Its entire attention is fixed on the light that has finally awoken. It spreads both hands, and black flame slowly forms a ring behind it. **"...Come."** **"...Prove..."** **"...that Ra was not wrong to choose you."**

For the first time, Fradonic steps forward. He still doesn't fully understand the power now running through him — but one thing he knows: if he steps back, everyone he loves dies. He grips his sword, then turns to the party. His expression is the same as it's always been — not the look of a legend, not a hero, only a friend. *"I..."* he says with a small smile, *"...still need your help."*

***Final Battle.*** The party's last fight against the **Avatar of Kael'zar** begins (full stats in *Appendix B: Creatures*). Fradonic fights alongside them using the power he's just inherited from Ra, but that power isn't stable yet — every time he overuses it, cracks of light spread further across his body, slowly wearing down his stamina. Sera calls out warnings more than once: *"Don't push yourself."* *"Your body isn't ready."* Fradonic only answers, looking at House Ra burning around him: *"If I stop..."* *"...who else will protect them?"*

***During the Battle.*** Use these beats to show that all of Soyo Gumi is still fighting, even from the sidelines. **Anthony** helps hold off attacks aimed at the party despite his grievous wounds. The **Chief of Soyo Gumi** keeps directing Nisshoku and Gyokko to evacuate the townsfolk still trapped in Atrakaj. The Gyokko Commanders carry the wounded clear of the fighting. The Nisshoku Commanders hold the perimeter, keeping any other fiend from reaching House Ra. None of them can face the Avatar of Kael'zar directly anymore — but they never stop fighting to protect the city.

***The Demon of Wrath.*** The longer the fight goes on, the clearer it becomes that the Avatar of Kael'zar isn't enjoying this battle. It's testing Fradonic — deliberately holding back its attacks at times, deliberately letting him recover at others. Sera notices it first, calling out: *"Fradonic!"* *"Don't take the bait!"* Kael'zar only laughs. **"...Good."** **"...Rise again."** **"...Show me..."** **"...how much of Ra's inheritance remains."**

***The Final Clash.*** As the Avatar of Kael'zar begins to weaken, the black flame wreathing its body starts to dim — yet its smile only grows wider. It looks at Fradonic, then laughs. **"...Enough."** Silence falls over the battlefield. The Avatar lowers both hands. For the first time, it stops attacking. **"...I've seen what I came to see."** Fradonic raises his sword. *"What do you mean?"* The Avatar looks straight at him. **"...I didn't come..."** **"...to take that light."** Silence returns to all of House Ra. Even Sera goes still. **"...I came..."** **"...to confirm..."** it points at Fradonic, **"...that Ra's successor..."** **"...truly exists."**

At once, the black flame wreathing the Avatar's body loses its shape. Great cracks spread across it. The black fire slowly turns to red embers, scattering on the wind. The Avatar laughs. **"...We'll..."** **"...meet again..."** Its gaze shifts to Sera. **"...Human Vaelithra."** Then back to Fradonic. **"...And when that day comes..."** **"...I won't send an avatar."** It tilts its head toward the sky. **"...Give Ra..."** **"...my regards."** A moment later, its whole body dissolves into flame that fades on the wind. Silence. For the first time since the Avatar's arrival, Atrakaj can hear the wind again. The battle... is over.

## Ending the Chapter

Kael'zar is gone — not defeated, but satisfied, and that distinction should sit uneasily with the party. House Ra is still standing, but only barely, and the price of that has been written across every fallen Sun Ray in the courtyard. Continue to *Part V: What the Sun Left Behind*.
